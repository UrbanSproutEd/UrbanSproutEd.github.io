Problem

& The Evidence

1

Children don’t know the sector that feeds them

  • Agriculture is around 23% of GDP and 37.4% of jobs (FAO)
  • Pakistan ranks 106th of 123 on the Global Hunger Index, and 4 in 10 children under five are stunted (UNICEF)
3

Agriculture is at high risk, and Sindh is on the front line

  • 2022 floods: 57% of Sindh’s cropland affected; estimated losses of 88% cotton, 80% rice, 61% sugarcane (Scientific Reports, 2023)
  • Pakistan was ranked the most climate-affected country in 2022 (Germanwatch)
  • Water per person fell from 5,600+ m³ to below 900 m³; agriculture uses 90%+ of freshwater; irrigation efficiency is below 45% (FreshPlaza)
  • 22% water shortage approved for Punjab and Sindh for Rabi 2026-27 (IRSA, Oct 2026)
  • Why urban farming: Sindh is ~54% urban (Pakistan ~39%), and urban population grows 3.65%/yr vs 1.90% in rural areas (PBS Census 2023)
2

Food is lost before it reaches the city

  • 20–40% of fruits and vegetables are lost after harvest (Economic Survey 2025-26)
  • Causes: limited cold storage, power outages, and poor transport (FreshPlaza, Dawn)
  • Hydroponics grows food near the consumer, so shorter routes mean less spoilage
Sources: FAO · UNICEF · Pakistan Economic Survey 2025-26 · Scientific Reports (2023) · Germanwatch CRI · IRSA · PBS Census 2023 · FreshPlaza · Dawn

RDWC

Recirculating Deep Water Culture

Space Required: 10 ft × 8 ft (80 sq ft)Total Capacity: 20 Plants (4 Rows × 5 Plants)Pipe Length: 6 ft

Main Components

  • 4 × 6 ft PVC growing pipes (3″ or 4″)
  • 20 × Net pots (2.5″ – 3″)
  • 1 × 50–70 L food-grade reservoir
  • Submersible water pump (800–1,200 L/h)
  • Air pump with 2–4 air stones
  • PVC fittings (elbows, tees, end caps)
  • Optional: Water chiller & automated timer

Operating Conditions

  • Water Temperature: 18–22°C (65–72°F)
  • pH Level: 5.5 – 6.5
  • EC Range: 1.2 – 2.0 mS/cm

Best Practices & Maintenance

  • Use a light-tight reflective tent with active airflow
  • Keep nutrient water strictly below 22°C to prevent root rot
  • Automate pump and lighting schedules
  • Flush and clean the system regularly

DWC System – Prototype

Hydroponics Deep Water Culture

Footprint: 40 × 30 × 25 cmCapacity: 30–40 LYield: 4–6 Plants

How It Works

  1. Plant roots grow directly in the nutrient solution (no soil is used).
  2. An air pump continuously provides oxygen directly to the roots via an air stone.
  3. Nutrient solution is kept still (with oxygen) or continuously circulated.
  4. Plants absorb water and nutrients directly, growing faster.

Ideal Conditions (DWC)

  • pH Level: 5.5 – 6.5
  • EC Range: 1.2 – 2.0 mS/cm
  • Water Temperature: 18 – 22°C (65–72°F)
  • Light Duration: 12 – 16 hrs/day

System Components Checklist

  • 1 × Plastic container with lid (30–40L)
  • 4–6 × Net pots (size-appropriate)
  • 1 × Air pump & Tubing
  • 1 × Air stone / Diffuser
  • Growing medium (LECA / coco pebbles)
  • Hydroponic nutrient solution
